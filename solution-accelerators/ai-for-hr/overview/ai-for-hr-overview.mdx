AI for HR gives employees one conversational front door to HR services that today span a leave portal, a benefits site, a payslip archive, a profile record, a case queue, and a timesheet tool. Employees ask in plain language, and the right specialist agent completes the task in your systems of record.

AI for HR ships as a Solution Accelerator: a multi-agent application that Kore.ai has already designed, built, and tested. You connect your systems, set your configuration values, and publish it to employees through AI for Work.

## What AI for HR does

AI for HR covers seven service areas across the employee experience. An entry-point supervisor reads each request and routes it to the specialist that owns that area.

| Service area | What employees do |
|--------------|-------------------|
| **Time off** | Check leave balances, view requests, apply for leave, and plan the longest break around holidays |
| **Benefits** | See available and enrolled plans, add dependents, and enroll |
| **Pay** | View payslip details and get totals for any pay period |
| **Profile** | View the employee record, update selected fields, and find colleagues |
| **Policies and cases** | Get grounded policy answers and raise, comment on, view, and resolve HR cases |
| **Resignation** | Submit a voluntary resignation and reach the exit survey |
| **Timesheets** | View timesheets for themselves or a direct report |

## How it works

AI for HR runs as a multi-agent application and reaches employees through a single published Autonomous Agent. The whole experience stays one conversation, whatever the request.

1. The employee asks in AI for Work, Microsoft Teams, Copilot, web, or voice.
2. HR_Supervisor identifies the HR intent, stores it in shared session memory, acknowledges the request, and hands it to exactly one specialist.
3. The specialist reads the stored context and calls its tools to fetch what it needs.
4. For any change, the specialist shows what it plans to do and waits for the employee to confirm.
5. The specialist completes the action in Workday or ServiceNow and reports the result.

For the tiers, the agent team, the HANDOFF model, and session memory, see the Solution architecture page.

## Systems of record

AI for HR acts directly on the systems your HR organization already runs. It holds no HR data of its own.

| System | What AI for HR uses it for |
|--------|-----------------------------|
| **Workday** | Leave, benefits, payroll, the employee master record, timesheets, and termination |
| **ServiceNow** | The HR service catalog and HR cases |
| **Search AI** | HR policy and handbook knowledge, scoped to India and the US |

## Principles that shape every answer

AI for HR applies the same rules in every service area. These rules keep employees in control and keep answers accurate.

- **Values come from the source.** Agents surface values verbatim from the system of record and never estimate or reword them.
- **Every write waits for a yes.** Read paths run freely, and every write action runs only after explicit in-chat confirmation.
- **Answers stay grounded.** Policy answers come from your indexed content with source links. When nothing matches, the agent offers to raise a case.
- **One owner per request.** The supervisor routes each request to exactly one specialist and never performs HR work itself.

## Who uses AI for HR

Four groups work with AI for HR, and each one gets a different benefit. Roll out to all four, or start with employees and managers.

| Audience | What they get |
|----------|---------------|
| **Employees** | Instant self-service for leave, benefits, pay, profile, and policy questions |
| **Managers** | Team information and direct report timesheets without opening an HR system |
| **HR teams** | Fewer routine queries and complete, structured cases for everything else |
| **Administrators** | One agentic app to configure, govern, and monitor |
