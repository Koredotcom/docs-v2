Pick your accelerator in minutes by answering three questions about your function, your systems, and your scope. Then run the fit check to confirm that AI for HR matches your environment before you plan the rollout.

Bring your HR, IT, or recruiting lead into this exercise. They know which requests fill the queue today, and those requests decide which accelerator pays off first.

## Start with three questions

Work through the questions in order. Each answer points you to an accelerator, an extension, or another Kore.ai path.

| Question | What It Decides |
|----------|-----------------|
| **1. Which function do you want to serve?** | The accelerator you start from |
| **2. Do your systems match its integrations?** | Whether you configure the tools as they are or point them at your own endpoints |
| **3. Does its scope cover your top requests?** | Whether you deploy the accelerator as is, extend it, or choose another path |

### Question 1: which function do you want to serve?

Each accelerator serves one business function through its own set of agentic apps. Match the function to the team that fields the most repetitive requests today.

| Your Top Requests | Accelerator |
|-------------------|-------------|
| Leave, benefits, pay, profile updates, and HR policies | **AI for HR** |
| Access, incidents, outages, and endpoint issues | **AI for IT** |
| Job descriptions, screening, and interviews | **AI for Recruiting** |
| Banking, healthcare, or retail workflows | **AI for Banking**, **AI for Healthcare**, or **AI for Retail** |

### Question 2: do your systems match its integrations?

An accelerator delivers value only when it reaches your systems of record. Check that your core systems appear in the list for your accelerator.

| Accelerator | Integrations |
|-------------|--------------|
| **AI for HR** | Workday and ServiceNow in the current implementation, with Search AI for policy knowledge. The product catalog also lists Dayforce, Oracle Fusion, SAP SuccessFactors, UKG, and CommunityWFM. |
| **AI for IT** | BMC, Certero, Freshservice, Jira Service Management, ServiceNow, Sophos, and TOPdesk |
| **AI for Recruiting** | ADP, BambooHR, Darwinbox, Greenhouse Recruiting, iCIMS, JazzHR, SmartRecruiters, and Workable |

A missing system doesn't rule an accelerator out. Every accelerator reaches its systems through HTTP tools, so your builder points those tools at your own endpoints on Agent Platform.

### Question 3: does its scope cover your top requests?

List the ten requests your team handles most often, then compare them with the accelerator's use cases. The overlap tells you how much you deploy as is and how much you extend.

| Overlap | What to Do |
|---------|------------|
| **Full** | Deploy the accelerator and configure it. |
| **Partial** | Deploy the accelerator, then add your own agents on Agent Platform for the gaps. |
| **Little or none** | Assemble a solution from AI Marketplace, or build on Agent Platform. |

## Map the employee lifecycle

The employee lifecycle runs through eight stages, and two accelerators share them. Find the stages that matter most to you and note which accelerator covers them.

| Stage | Agentic Apps That Support It | Accelerator |
|-------|------------------------------|-------------|
| **1. Attract** | Job description management, Position creation | AI for Recruiting |
| **2. Screen** | Candidate screening | AI for Recruiting |
| **3. Interview** | Interview scheduling, Interview transcription and feedback, Candidate experience | AI for Recruiting |
| **4. Onboard** | Onboarding, Task management | AI for HR |
| **5. Integrate** | Profile management, Benefit management, Search/HR help desk | AI for HR |
| **6. Develop** | Talent capabilities such as learning recommendations, performance reviews, and development goals | AI for HR |
| **7. Engage** | Absence management, Compensation, Case management, Moments that matter | AI for HR |
| **8. Transition** | Offboarding | AI for HR |

AI for Recruiting takes a candidate up to the offer, and AI for HR takes over from the first day onward. Deploy both when you own the full journey.

## Run the AI for HR fit check

Review each statement and mark the ones that describe your organization today. Every statement that holds true removes a customization task from your implementation plan.

| Statement | Why It Matters |
|-----------|----------------|
| Workday holds your leave, benefits, payroll, employee records, timesheets, and terminations. | Six of the seven specialists read from and write to Workday. |
| ServiceNow runs your HR service catalog and HR cases. | Case Management creates and manages cases in ServiceNow. |
| Your HR policies live in a repository that Search AI connects to, such as Confluence Cloud. | Every policy answer comes from content that Search AI indexes. |
| Your employees need policy answers for India or the US. | Case Management searches policies for these two regions. |
| You use AI for Work, or you plan to. | Employees reach AI for HR through an Autonomous Agent in AI for Work. |
| You want employees to confirm every change before it reaches your HR systems. | Every write action in AI for HR waits for the employee's confirmation. |

<Tip>Share the fit check with your HR system owners early. Their answers shape your scope, your timeline, and the access you request during planning.</Tip>

## Choose another path when you need to

An accelerator fits best when your function and systems match it closely. Switch paths when you need a different starting point.

| If You Want To | Use |
|----------------|-----|
| Deploy a complete app for a business function | A Solution Accelerator |
| Assemble your own solution from agents, templates, and integrations | AI Marketplace |
| Build an app that no accelerator covers | Agent Platform |
