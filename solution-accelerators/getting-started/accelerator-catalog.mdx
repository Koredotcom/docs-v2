## AI for HR

AI for HR gives employees one conversational front door to leave, benefits, pay, profile, HR cases, resignation, and timesheets. It covers more than 40 use cases and includes more than 10 customizable agentic app templates.

Employees, managers, and HR teams use AI for HR to search policies and complete HR tasks on their own. The assistant routes every request to a specialist agent, which reads from your HR systems freely and writes to them only after the employee confirms.

### Agents

One supervisor and seven specialists make up the AI for HR team. Each specialist owns one domain and reaches its system through dedicated tools.

| Agent | Domain | System |
|-------|--------|--------|
| **HR_Supervisor** | Detects intent and routes every request | None |
| **Time Off Assistant** | Leave balances, requests, and leave planning | Workday |
| **Benefit Management** | Plans, enrollment, eligibility, and dependents | Workday |
| **Compensation** | Payslip questions for the logged-in employee | Workday |
| **Profile Management** | Viewing and updating the employee record | Workday |
| **Case Management** | HR policy answers and HR cases | Search AI, ServiceNow |
| **Offboarding Management** | Voluntary resignation | Workday |
| **Timesheet Management** | Viewing timesheets for yourself or a direct report | Workday |

The product catalog groups AI for HR into 13 agentic apps. Each app lists the tasks it handles and the systems it works with.

| Agentic App | What It Handles | Works With |
|-------------|-----------------|------------|

| **Document request** | Employment and benefits letters, and document requests | ServiceNow |

| **Offboarding** | Resignations and exit surveys | SAP, Workday |

See **Supported use cases** for what each app does and which agent delivers it.

### Channels

Employees reach AI for HR wherever they already work. Offer the AI for Work assistant, Microsoft Teams, Copilot, web, or voice, on desktop and mobile.

## AI for Recruiting

AI for Recruiting supports hiring from the first job description to the final interview. It connects to the applicant tracking and HR systems your recruiters use every day.

| Category | Items |
|----------|-------|
| **Agentic apps** | Candidate experience, Candidate screening, Interview scheduling, Interview transcription and feedback, Job description management, Position creation |
| **Integrations** | ADP, BambooHR, Darwinbox, Greenhouse Recruiting, iCIMS, JazzHR, SmartRecruiters, Workable |

<Tip>AI for Recruiting and AI for HR together cover the full employee lifecycle, from attracting candidates to an employee's final day. Plan them together if you own both hiring and HR operations.</Tip>
