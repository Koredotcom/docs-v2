---
title: Knowledge base connector integrations
sidebarTitle: Overview
---

[Connectors](/agent-platform/knowledge#connectors) bring content from third-party systems into a knowledge base so agents can retrieve it during conversations. Setting up a connector requires authentication credentials, content and filter selection, field mapping to the knowledge base schema, and access permission configuration.
