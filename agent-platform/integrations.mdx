Artemis connects your agents to external systems so they can retrieve data, take action, hand off conversations to live agents, and reach users across your enterprise ecosystem. Each integration is a configured, authenticated connection to a third-party system that your agents and workflows use.

The platform organizes integrations into five types. Each page lists the integrations of that type and links to the setup guide for each one.

| Type | What it covers |
|:-----|:---------------|
| [Kore.ai CX modules](/agent-platform/integrations/kore-cx-modules) | Kore.ai products that work with Artemis agents: CCAI, Campaign, Quality AI, Agent AI, and Agent Assist. |
| [Enterprise systems](/agent-platform/integrations/enterprise-systems) | Business applications that workflows use through connector actions, such as Slack, Gmail, Jira, and Salesforce. |
| [Contact centers](/agent-platform/integrations/contact-centers) | Agent desktops that receive a conversation when your agent hands off to a live agent. |
| [Channels](/agent-platform/channels) | Messaging, voice, SDK, webhook, and protocol surfaces where users reach your agents. |
| [Knowledge sources](/agent-platform/integrations/knowledge-sources) | Connectors that bring content from external systems into a knowledge base. |
