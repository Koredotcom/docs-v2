AI for HR cuts the time employees spend chasing HR answers and the time HR teams spend on routine requests. It gives every employee one place to ask, and it gives HR a consistent, always-on service that scales with the workforce.

Use this page to build your business case. It covers the shift AI for HR creates, the outcomes to expect, the value for each role, the KPIs to track, and the results customers report.

## From six HR tools to one conversation

Employees today visit a different tool for every HR task. AI for HR replaces those visits with one conversation in the place they already work.

| Before AI for HR | With AI for HR |
|------------------|----------------|
| Leave portal, benefits site, payslip archive, profile record, HR case queue, and timesheet tool | One conversation in AI for Work. The employee asks once, the supervisor routes the request, and the right specialist completes it in Workday or ServiceNow. |

Each switch between tools costs the employee time and context. One conversation removes those switches, and the assistant carries the context from one request to the next.

## Outcomes you can expect

AI for HR delivers four outcomes that HR leaders track closely. Each outcome ties back to a specific capability in the accelerator.

- **Lower HR workload.** Specialist agents complete routine requests end to end, such as leave requests, payslip questions, and profile updates. Your HR team spends its time on the cases that need human judgment.
- **Self-service around the clock.** Employees and managers find policies and complete tasks on their own, at any hour, in any channel.
- **Better SLA compliance.** The assistant answers policy questions instantly. When it can't answer, it raises a case with a complete, structured description, so your team starts with everything it needs.
- **A consistent employee experience.** Every employee gets the same accurate answer, whether they ask in the AI for Work assistant, Microsoft Teams, Copilot, web, or voice.

## Value for every role

AI for HR serves four groups, and each one gains something different. Share these points with each stakeholder group when you present the business case.

### Employees

Employees get instant help with everyday HR needs. They stop waiting on HR for answers they can get in seconds.

- Check leave balances, apply for leave, and plan longer breaks.
- Review benefits, add dependents, and enroll.
- See payslip details and pay totals for any period.
- Get policy answers with links to the source.

### Managers

Managers handle team matters quickly, even from a phone between meetings. They spend less time in HR systems and more time with their teams.

- Review a direct report's timesheet for any date range.
- Find colleagues and team members in the directory.
- Track team requests without switching systems.

### HR teams

HR teams offload routine queries and apply policy the same way for everyone. They serve the whole workforce around the clock without adding headcount.

- Deflect routine questions to self-service.
- Receive complete, structured cases in ServiceNow.
- Keep answers aligned with the policies you index.

### Help desk agents

Help desk agents resolve the remaining issues faster. AI surfaces relevant steps and past fixes while it handles triage and status updates.

## Trust built into every answer

Value depends on accuracy, and AI for HR enforces it by design. These four rules let you roll out self-service without adding risk to your HR data.

| Rule | What It Guarantees |
|------|--------------------|
| **Confirm first** | No change reaches your HR systems until the employee confirms. |
| **Source values** | Agents show values exactly as Workday or ServiceNow returns them. |
| **Cited answers** | Policy answers link to their source documents. |
| **Own data only** | Pay answers cover the logged-in employee only. |

## KPIs to track

Pick the KPIs that match your rollout scope and measure a baseline before you go live. Then track the same KPIs after launch to prove the value.

| KPI | What It Tells You |
|-----|-------------------|
| **HR ticket volume** | How many requests self-service absorbs |
| **Ticket resolution time** | How fast HR and help desk teams close the cases that remain |
| **Benefits enrollment completion rate** | How many employees finish enrollment on time |
| **Onboarding time** | How quickly new hires become productive |
| **Employee productivity** | How much time employees save on HR tasks |

Follow three steps to measure each KPI:

1. Record the baseline from your current HR systems before launch.
2. Track the KPI in AI for Work workspace analytics and your HR systems after launch.
3. Compare the two and report the change to your stakeholders.

## Why AI for Work and AI for HR together

AI for Work gives AI for HR its reach, and AI for HR gives AI for Work its HR depth. Together they deliver three advantages.

- **Integration:** one assistant works across the enterprise systems your teams already run.
- **End-to-end automation:** one solution covers HR workflows across the employee lifecycle.
