AI for HR lets employees and managers handle leave, benefits, pay, profile updates, HR cases, resignation, and timesheets in one conversation. Seven specialist agents deliver these use cases, and each agent owns one domain from question to completed transaction.

This page walks you through every domain: what employees can do, how the agent completes the task, and prompts you can try right away in AI for Work.

## How AI for HR handles every request

Every use case follows the same five steps, whatever the domain. Learn this pattern once, and you can predict what the assistant asks and when it acts.

1. **Ask.** The employee types a request in plain language.
2. **Route.** HR_Supervisor detects the intent and hands the request to one specialist.
3. **Gather.** The specialist fetches the data it needs and asks only for what's missing.
4. **Confirm.** Before any change, the specialist shows what it plans to do and waits for a yes.
5. **Act.** The specialist completes the request in Workday or ServiceNow and reports the result.

Read-only requests, such as a balance check, skip the confirmation step. If the employee changes topic midway, the specialist hands the conversation to the right peer and takes it back afterward.

## Time off

The Time Off Assistant takes leave from question to submitted request, with no trip to the leave portal. It validates balance, eligibility, holidays, and weekends before it submits anything.

### What you can do

The assistant resolves every leave request to one of four flows. Employees describe what they want, and the assistant picks the flow.

| Use Case | What Happens |
|----------|--------------|
| **Check a leave balance** | Shows the remaining balance for every leave type. |
| **View existing requests** | Lists all active leave requests. |
| **Apply for leave** | Collects the dates and leave type, validates them, confirms, and submits. |
| **Plan the longest break** | Proposes dates that bridge weekends and company holidays, for the longest continuous break with the fewest leave days. |

### How it works

The assistant always starts from today's system date and never guesses a date. It then tags every day in the range, because only working days consume leave.

1. Get today's date.
2. Fetch the leave balance and the company holiday calendar.
3. Tag each date in the range as HOLIDAY, WEEKEND, or WORKING.
4. Show the summary and wait for the employee to confirm.
5. Create the request in Workday.

<Tip>**Try it:** "How much PTO do I have left this year?" Then: "I want to apply for leaves." When the assistant asks for dates, reply "December 1 to December 19".</Tip>

## Benefits

Benefit Management guides employees through coverage, enrollment, eligibility, and dependents. It answers follow-up questions about an enrolled plan from the data it already fetched, so replies stay fast.

### What you can do

Employees explore their options and enroll without leaving the conversation. The agent collects every detail the enrollment needs before it submits.

| Use Case | What Happens |
|----------|--------------|
| **See available plans** | Shows the benefit plans the employee qualifies for. |
| **See enrolled plans** | Shows each plan the employee already holds, with its key details. |
| **Add a dependent** | Collects the first name, gender, date of birth, relationship, address, and country. |
| **Enroll in a plan** | Submits the enrollment with the selected plan and coverage targets after the employee confirms. |

### How it works

An enrollment moves through five steps, and the employee stays in control at each one. Nothing reaches Workday until the employee confirms.

1. Show the available plans.
2. The employee picks a plan.
3. Select existing dependents, or add a new one.
4. Review the summary and confirm.
5. Enroll in Workday.

<Tip>**Try it:** "Which benefit plans am I enrolled in?" Then ask a follow-up about one of the plans.</Tip>

## Pay

Compensation answers pay questions for the logged-in employee only, straight from payslip records. It never modifies payroll, issues refunds, or resolves pay disputes.

### What you can do

Employees get either the detail or the total, depending on how they ask. The agent reads the wording and picks the right mode automatically.

| Mode | When the Agent Uses It | What the Employee Sees |
|------|------------------------|------------------------|
| **VIEW** | The employee asks to see a payslip. | A field by field breakdown of each payslip in the period. |
| **AGGREGATE** | The employee uses totaling words, such as "total" or "sum". | Computed totals, such as gross pay, deductions, and net pay. |

### How it works

The agent first pins down the pay period, then fetches only the payslips in that range. An aggregation workflow does all the arithmetic, so every total matches the source data exactly.

1. Resolve the pay period from the message, or ask for the missing start or end.
2. Search the payslips for that range.
3. Pick VIEW or AGGREGATE based on the wording.
4. Show the fields, or the totals that the aggregation workflow returns.

<Tip>**Try it:** "Hey, can you tell me my total earnings and deductions for the last three months of 2020?"</Tip>

## Profile

Profile Management shows the employee record in a clear table and updates selected fields on request. It confirms every change in a fixed format and asks for supporting documents on sensitive fields.

### What you can do

Employees keep their own record current and find colleagues in seconds. The agent always shows friendly labels, never raw field names.

| Use Case | What Happens |
|----------|--------------|
| **View my profile** | Shows the employee's details in a structured table. |
| **Update a field** | Identifies the field and new value, confirms, normalizes the value, and applies it. |
| **Find an employee** | Searches the directory by name and returns the person's details. |

<Tip>**Try it:** "I need to update my phone number."</Tip>

## Policies and cases

Case Management answers HR policy questions with source links and runs HR cases from creation to resolution. It infers the employee's region from the message and searches policies for India or the US.

### What you can do

The agent classifies every request into one of six intents, then runs the matching flow. Employees get an answer when one exists and a case when it doesn't.

| Intent | What Happens |
|--------|--------------|
| **hr_query** | Searches the HR knowledge index and answers with source links. |
| **create_case** | Fetches the HR service catalog, collects the title, description, and priority, and creates the case. |
| **add_comment** | Adds a comment to an existing case. |
| **view_comments** | Shows the comments on a case. |
| **view_incident** | Shows the details of a case. |
| **resolve_case** | Resolves a case. |

### How it works

Policy questions always go to the knowledge index first. The agent offers a case only when it finds nothing, so employees never get a made-up answer.

1. Search the HR knowledge index for the question.
2. When a match exists, answer with source links.
3. When nothing matches, offer to raise a case in ServiceNow, collect the details, and create it after the employee confirms.

Case requests, such as adding a comment, skip the search and go straight to their flow.

<Tip>**Try it:** "US employee leave policy" Then: "I'm having trouble with a travel reimbursement. Can you help?"</Tip>

## Resignation

Offboarding Management walks employees through a voluntary resignation with a supportive, empathetic tone. It acts only on what the employee states and never assumes intent.

1. Fetch the valid resignation reasons.
2. Collect the termination date.
3. Show the summary and wait for confirmation.
4. Submit the resignation and verify that it succeeded.
5. Point the employee to the exit survey.

<Note>Offboarding Management handles voluntary resignation only. Route involuntary terminations through your standard HR process.</Note>

## Timesheets

Timesheet Management shows timesheet entries for the employee or a direct report across a date range. It confirms the range before it fetches anything.

- **View your own timesheet** for any date range.
- **View a direct report's timesheet**, if you manage that employee.

The agent stays view-only. It never creates, edits, or deletes timesheet entries.

## Combine tasks in one request

Employees can ask for several things in a single message. AI for Work shows the planned sequence first, and the employee starts it or edits it.

<Tip>**Try it:** "I'll be on PTO tomorrow. Please reschedule my meeting with James to the day after tomorrow. Also, gather any tasks assigned to me that are due tomorrow, summarize them, and send the summary to Lucy via email. Finally, apply my leave for the day."</Tip>

AI for Work plans this request in four steps:

1. Reschedule the meeting with James.
2. Find the tasks due tomorrow.
3. Summarize the tasks.
4. Email the summary to Lucy.
