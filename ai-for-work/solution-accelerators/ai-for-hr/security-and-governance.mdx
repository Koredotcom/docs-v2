AI for HR handles leave, pay, benefits, and personal records, so governance decisions matter as much as configuration. Set your controls in AI for Work and Agent Platform before you invite employees.

This page covers access, data handling, guardrails, orchestration, and monitoring. Agree each setting with your security team and record the decision.

## Access control

Decide who reaches AI for HR and who administers it. The workspace settings in AI for Work carry these controls.

| Control | What you decide |
|---------|-----------------|
| **User enrollment** | Open to your domain, invitation only, or synced from your directory |
| **Workspace roles** | Who creates, edits, publishes, and monitors agents |
| **SSO** | Which identity provider authenticates employees |
| **IP restrictions** | Which networks reach the workspace |
| **Knowledge access** | Which employees see which indexed content, through role-based access in Search AI |

## Data handling

AI for HR reads and writes data in your systems and keeps none of its own. Two settings govern what the platform retains and what it masks.

| Control | What you decide |
|---------|-----------------|
| **PII guardrails** | Which personal data to redact or replace, such as Social Security numbers, email addresses, phone numbers, credit card numbers, and PAN numbers |
| **Data retention** | Whether to retain full queries or metadata only, set by account or by agent |

Identity flows through two mechanisms. Config variables hold identity values for the HTTP tools, and AI for Work passes the employee's email to the agentic app as metadata.

## Guardrails

Guardrails keep the assistant inside the boundaries your policies set. Configure them at workspace level, so every agent inherits them.

- **Banned topics.** List the subjects the assistant must refuse.
- **PII masking.** Apply the redaction and replacement rules you agreed above.
- **Compliance posture.** The platform supports SOC 2 and GDPR adherence.

## Orchestration settings

Orchestration steps decide how AI for Work handles each query before and after the agents run. Enable only the steps your deployment needs.

| Step | What it does |
|------|--------------|
| **Guardrail enforcement** | Applies your banned topics and safety rules. |
| **Small talk handling** | Answers conversational messages without routing them. |
| **Intelligent agent routing** | Sends the query to the right agent. |
| **Enterprise knowledge lookup** | Searches indexed content for an answer. |
| **Fallback to AI knowledge** | Answers from the model when nothing else applies. |

<Note>Review the fallback setting carefully. AI for HR grounds policy answers in your indexed content, and a broad fallback can return answers your HR team hasn't approved.</Note>

## Safety rules built into the agents

The accelerator enforces four rules in every domain, whatever your workspace settings. They limit what a misdirected request can do.

- Every write action waits for explicit in-chat confirmation.
- Agents surface values verbatim from the system of record.
- Policy answers come from indexed content with source links.
- Compensation serves the logged-in employee only.

## Monitoring and audit

Assign an owner for monitoring before go-live. Two surfaces give you the picture.

| Surface | What it shows |
|---------|---------------|
| **Workspace analytics** | Total queries, active users, tokens used, queries per agent, and top agents |
| **Audit logs** | User actions and agent activity, for investigation and accountability |
| **Multi-agent trace** | Every agent, model call, and tool run in a session, with status and duration |

## Governance checklist

Close each item before employees reach the assistant. Record the owner and the decision alongside it.

| Item | Owner |
|------|-------|
| Enrollment method agreed | AI for Work admin |
| Workspace roles assigned | AI for Work admin |
| PII guardrails configured | Security team |
| Banned topics listed | Security team with HR |
| Data retention set | Security team |
| Orchestration steps enabled or disabled | AI for Work admin |
| Knowledge access rules applied | HR knowledge owner |
| Monitoring owner named | HR operations |
