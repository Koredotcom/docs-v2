## Why use an accelerator

A Solution Accelerator hands you the hardest parts of an agentic app on day one: routing, agent handoffs, shared context, system tools, and safety rules. You skip months of design work and spend your time on configuration, content, and rollout.

This section shows the problem accelerators solve, what changes when you deploy one, and what you still own. Read it to decide whether to start from an accelerator or build from scratch.

### The problem: tool sprawl and routine work

Employees lose hours every week to scattered tools and repetitive tasks. These four numbers show the scale of the problem.

| Measure | Finding |
|---------|---------|
| **Application switching** | About half of businesses use an average of 17 standalone work tools. |
| **Information search** | Employees spend 20 to 30% of their time searching for information. |
| **Routine tasks** | 40% of employee time goes to tasks that automation can handle. |
| **Employee burnout** | Burnout costs an estimated $322 billion globally in turnover and lost productivity. |

Every extra tool adds a login, a new interface, and another place to search. Routine requests pile up in HR and IT queues, and specialists spend their day on work that software can do.

### What changes with an accelerator

An accelerator answers each part of the problem with a specific capability. Here is what shifts for your employees and your support teams.

- **From switching apps to one entry point.** Employees ask in AI for Work, and a single request reaches the right agent and system.
- **From searching to asking.** Employees get answers from your documents through agentic RAG and more than 100 connectors, with source links and role-based access.
- **From routine tickets to completed tasks.** Specialist agents finish requests end to end, such as leave requests, payslip questions, and profile updates.
- **From overloaded queues to focused teams.** Self-service absorbs routine requests, and the rest arrive as complete, structured cases.

### Build from scratch or start from an accelerator

Every multi-agent app needs the same building blocks. Compare what you design yourself with what AI for HR already gives you.

| Building Block | Build From Scratch | Start From AI for HR |
|----------------|--------------------|----------------------|
| **Intent routing** | Design intent categories and routing rules. | HR_Supervisor routes seven intent categories out of the box. |
| **Agent handoffs** | Wire every agent to every other agent. | Seven supervisor handoffs and 42 peer handoffs, already connected. |
| **Shared context** | Design a memory schema. | Session memory with `current_intent` and `target_agent` in every agent. |
| **System tools** | Build and test each API call. | Ready HTTP and workflow tools for Workday, ServiceNow, and Search AI. |
| **Safety rules** | Write and test confirmation logic. | Every write waits for the employee's confirmation. |
| **Deployment** | Design the employee entry point. | Publish to AI for Work as one Autonomous Agent. |

Accelerators stay flexible. AI for HR includes more than 10 customizable agentic app templates, and you extend it with your own agents on Agent Platform whenever you need to.

### What you still own

An accelerator removes design work, not your ownership of data and access. Plan for these tasks from the start, and your rollout stays on schedule.

- Grant API access to your systems of record, such as Workday and ServiceNow.
- Set the config variables that the tools read at run time.
- Index your HR policies in Search AI and keep them current.
- Add your LLM credentials and pick the default model.
- Set governance in AI for Work: user enrollment, guardrails, data retention, and orchestration.

<Tip>Keep your HR policy content current after launch. The assistant answers only from what you index, so stale content leads to stale answers.</Tip>

## Choose the right accelerator

Pick your accelerator in minutes by answering three questions about your function, your systems, and your scope. Then run the fit check to confirm that AI for HR matches your environment before you plan the rollout.

Bring your HR, IT, or recruiting lead into this exercise. They know which requests fill the queue today, and those requests decide which accelerator pays off first.

### Start with three questions

Work through the questions in order. Each answer points you to an accelerator, an extension, or another Kore.ai path.

| Question | What It Decides |
|----------|-----------------|
| **1. Which function do you want to serve?** | The accelerator you start from |
| **2. Do your systems match its integrations?** | Whether you configure the tools as they are or point them at your own endpoints |
| **3. Does its scope cover your top requests?** | Whether you deploy the accelerator as is, extend it, or choose another path |

#### Question 1: Which function do you want to serve?

Each accelerator serves one business function through its own set of agentic apps. Match the function to the team that fields the most repetitive requests today.

| Your Top Requests | Accelerator |
|-------------------|-------------|
| Leave, benefits, pay, profile updates, and HR policies | **AI for HR** |
| Access, incidents, outages, and endpoint issues | **AI for IT** |
| Job descriptions, screening, and interviews | **AI for Recruiting** |
| Banking, healthcare, or retail workflows | **AI for Banking**, **AI for Healthcare**, or **AI for Retail** |

#### Question 2: Does your system match its integrations?

An accelerator delivers value only when it reaches your systems of record. Check that your core systems appear in the list for your accelerator.

| Accelerator | Integrations |
|-------------|--------------|
| **AI for HR** | Workday and ServiceNow in the current implementation, with Search AI for policy knowledge. The product catalog also lists Dayforce, Oracle Fusion, SAP SuccessFactors, UKG, and CommunityWFM. |
| **AI for IT** | BMC, Certero, Freshservice, Jira Service Management, ServiceNow, Sophos, and TOPdesk |
| **AI for Recruiting** | ADP, BambooHR, Darwinbox, Greenhouse Recruiting, iCIMS, JazzHR, SmartRecruiters, and Workable |

A missing system doesn't rule an accelerator out. Every accelerator reaches its systems through HTTP tools, so your builder points those tools at your own endpoints on Agent Platform.

#### Question 3: Does its scope cover your top requests?

List the ten requests your team handles most often, then compare them with the accelerator's use cases. The overlap tells you how much you deploy as is and how much you extend.

| Overlap | What to Do |
|---------|------------|
| **Full** | Deploy the accelerator and configure it. |
| **Partial** | Deploy the accelerator, then add your own agents on Agent Platform for the gaps. |
| **Little or none** | Assemble a solution from AI Marketplace, or build on Agent Platform. |

### Run the AI for HR fit check

Review each statement and mark the ones that describe your organization today. Every statement that holds true removes a customization task from your implementation plan.

| Statement | Why It Matters |
|-----------|----------------|
| Workday holds your leave, benefits, payroll, employee records, timesheets, and terminations. | Six of the seven specialists read from and write to Workday. |
| ServiceNow runs your HR service catalog and HR cases. | Case Management creates and manages cases in ServiceNow. |
| Your HR policies live in a repository that Search AI connects to, such as Confluence Cloud. | Every policy answer comes from content that Search AI indexes. |
| Your employees need policy answers for India or the US. | Case Management searches policies for these two regions. |
| You use AI for Work, or you plan to. | Employees reach AI for HR through an Autonomous Agent in AI for Work. |
| You want employees to confirm every change before it reaches your HR systems. | Every write action in AI for HR waits for the employee's confirmation. |

<Tip>Share the fit check with your HR system owners early. Their answers shape your scope, your timeline, and the access you request during planning.</Tip>

### AI for HR

AI for HR gives employees one conversational front door to leave, benefits, pay, profile, HR cases, resignation, and timesheets. It covers more than 40 use cases and includes more than 10 customizable agentic app templates.

Employees, managers, and HR teams use AI for HR to search policies and complete HR tasks on their own. The assistant routes every request to a specialist agent, which reads from your HR systems freely and writes to them only after the employee confirms.

One supervisor and seven specialists make up the AI for HR team. Each specialist owns one domain and reaches its system through dedicated tools.

| **Time Off Assistant** | Leave balances, requests, and leave planning | Workday |

| **Compensation** | Payslip questions for the logged-in employee | Workday |
